# Product

## Product Purpose
Block Island Hope for Jamaica is a charity foundation website that tells the story of practical outreach work across Jamaican communities. It serves as the public face of the organization, connecting supporters with mission stories, impact data, and ways to take action.

## Register
brand

## Users
- **Donors and supporters**: People considering financial or in-kind contributions. They need to trust the organization and see where their support goes.
- **Volunteers**: Individuals looking to serve on outreach teams, logistics, meal prep, or communications.
- **Partner organizations**: Churches, businesses, and institutions exploring sponsorship or co-hosting opportunities.
- **Community members**: Jamaican families and leaders who want to understand what the foundation does and how to connect.

## Brand Voice
Warm, grounded, dignified. Not performative charity language. Speaks plainly about real work with real communities. Avoids corporate nonprofit jargon. The tone is compassionate but direct, like a trusted community leader explaining what happened and what's next.

## Anti-References
- Generic nonprofit templates with stock photos of children
- Slick SaaS-style charity sites that feel corporate
- Overly emotional poverty-porn imagery or language
- AI-generated landing pages with purple gradients

## Design Principles
1. **Dignity first**: Every photo, word, and layout choice should respect the communities being served.
2. **Story over statistics**: Lead with narrative and place, not dashboards and charts.
3. **Grounded warmth**: The palette is nature-inspired (forest greens, warm earth tones, cream). It should feel like Jamaica, not like a tech startup.
4. **Clarity over cleverness**: Information architecture should be obvious. A first-time visitor should know what the org does within 5 seconds.
5. **Real over polished**: Authentic field photography over stock. Imperfect moments over staged ones.
